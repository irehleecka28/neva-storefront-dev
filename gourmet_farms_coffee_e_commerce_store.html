Our Heritage

Rooted in Silang, Cavite Since 1980

Gourmet Farms is the first organic farm in the Philippines. For over four decades, our coffee master roasters have been carefully selecting and micro-roasting the finest coffee beans from local farmers across Cavite, Batangas, and the highland mountains of Mindanao.

From our iconic batches of Kapeng Barako to delicate high-altitude Arabicas, every pouch is freshly packed in high-barrier bags with one-way aroma degassing valves to ensure peak freshness at your doorstep.
