Brewing Guides

Sip It Like You Mean It

1

Grind Fresh Beans

Use medium-coarse grind for French Press or medium-fine for Pour-Over methods.

2

Optimal Water Temp

Heat clean filtered water to 92°C–96°C (about 30 seconds after boiling).

3

Perfect Golden Ratio

We recommend 1:15 ratio — 15 grams of Gourmet Farms coffee per 225ml of water.
